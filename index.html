About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to BmiLoom, an Android app that calculates body mass index (BMI), published by Chrimson Edge Associates ("we", "us", "our"). It describes how information is handled when you use the app.

BmiLoom is designed to work entirely on your device. We built it so that we do not need your personal information in order to provide it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the app has no accounts)
  • Your height, weight, BMI results or any other health or fitness information
  • Your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to us or to anyone else

The developer has no server that receives data from the app.

What stays on your device

To work as described, the app keeps a small amount of information in its private storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device through the app.

Stored itemPurposeWhen it is created
Saved BMI records: date and time, height, weight and BMIShows your History, lets you reopen results and compare two recordsOnly when you tap Save result
Last entered height and weightFills the calculator with your previous values so you can recalculate quicklyWhen you enter valid values or calculate
Preferences: theme, default height unit, default weight unitRemembers how you like the app to look and behaveWhen you change them in Settings

This data is kept in the app's own storage area, which other apps cannot normally read. Android backup is turned off for BmiLoom, so this data is not included in automatic app backups.

Permissions

BmiLoom does not request any Android permissions. In particular, it does not ask for internet, network, location, storage, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Third parties

BmiLoom does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting services, and it does not connect to third-party servers. No information about you is provided to any third party by the app.

Your use of Google Play to download or update the app is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not control or receive the information Google collects through its services.

Your control

You decide what is saved and for how long.

  • Delete one record: open History and use the delete button on that record.
  • Delete all records: use Clear all in History, or Clear calculation history in Settings.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Android settings. This removes saved records, your last entered values and your preferences.

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, export or delete it for you. All of it is in your hands on your device.

Children

BmiLoom is intended for adults. The BMI categories used in the app apply to adults aged 20 and above, and the app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Health information notice

BMI is a screening measure, not a medical diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat and may not be appropriate for every person. BmiLoom does not provide medical advice. Please speak with a qualified healthcare professional about your health, weight or nutrition.

Security

Since the app does not transmit your information, the main protection is that it never leaves your phone. Saved records are stored in the app's private storage. Keeping your device secure, for example with a screen lock and system updates, helps protect any data stored on it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a future version of the app were to collect any information, this policy would be updated before that version is released, and the Data safety section on Google Play would be updated to match.
